# Statutory Source Index & Legislative References

> Authoritative inventory of Primary Acts, Devolved Statutory Instruments (UK SIs, SSIs, WSIs), and VOA Rating Manuals governing the Business Rates Engine across England, Wales, and Scotland (1990–2027).

## 1. Primary Legislation & Core Rating Acts

| Act & Citation | Nations | Core Provisions & Rating Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **Local Government Finance Act 1988 (c. 41)** | England, Wales | Foundation of national non-domestic rating; s.43 (occupied liability), s.44A (part-occupied apportionments), s.45 (unoccupied liability), s.47 (discretionary relief), s.57A (transitional relief power), s.64 (hereditament definition). |
| **Non-Domestic Rating Act 2023 (c. 53)** | England, Wales | English 3-year revaluation cycle; SBRR modernization; s.1 & Sched 1 (Improvement Relief / G-factors); s.2 (Heat Network Relief); high-value multiplier (RV $\ge £500\text{k}$); s.15 (13-week empty rate reset). |
| **Local Government Finance (Wales) Act 2024 (asc 6)** | Wales | Welsh 3-year revaluation synchronization; differential multipliers ($M \times N$); bilingual audit trails; anti-avoidance powers. |
| **Non-Domestic Rates (Scotland) Act 2020 (asp 4)** | Scotland | Scottish 3-year revaluation cycle; Valuation Roll transfer rules; statutory 3-tier multiplier framework (Basic, Intermediate, Higher). |
| **Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975 (c. 30)** | Scotland | Foundational Scottish rating statute; ss.7, 7A & 7B (rate-setting & differential multipliers); s.24 (unoccupied property rating); s.24A (part-occupied apportionment certificates). |
| **Local Government (Financial Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1962 (c. 9)** | Scotland | s.4 (mandatory 80% charitable relief & local authority discretionary top-ups). |
| **Local Government Act 2003 (c. 26)** | England, Wales | s.61 (inserts LGFA 1988 s.43(4A) & Sched 4ZA: SBRR); s.63 (rural settlement relief); s.64 (discretionary rate relief). |
| **Rating (Empty Properties) Act 2007 (c. 9)** | England, Wales | Amends LGFA 1988 s.45 establishing 100% unoccupied liability following rate-free exemption periods. |
| **Localism Act 2011 (c. 20)** | England, Wales | s.69 (expands LGFA 1988 s.47 discretionary powers to grant local discounts; statutory mechanism for RHL and SSBR schemes). |
| **Islands (Scotland) Act 2018 (asp 12)** | Scotland | Statutory island definition conferring 100% RHL relief up to £110k statutory cash cap. |
| **Rating (Coronavirus and Public Lavatories) Act 2021 (c. 12)** | England, Wales | 100% statutory rating exemption for standalone public conveniences. |
| **Local Government and Rating Act 1997 (c. 29)** | England, Wales | Rural commercial settlement relief framework under LGFA 1988 s.43(6A)–(6E). |
| **Telecommunications Infrastructure (Relief from NDR) Act 2018 (c. 1)** | England, Scotland | 100% mandatory relief for new fibre telecommunications infrastructure (LGFA 1988 s.43(4E); SSI 2019/111); Wales explicitly excluded under s.43(4E). |
| **Civic Government (Scotland) Act 1982 / SSI 2022/86** | Scotland | s.44: statutory short-term let licensing mandatory for self-catering to qualify for Scottish RHL (SSI 2026/69) & 15% TR cap (SSI 2026/135). |

### 3.2 Small Business Schemes (SBRR, SBBS & SSBR)

| Scheme | Nation | Taper Thresholds & Formulas | Multiple Property & Grace Rules | Statutory Authority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SBRR** | England | 100% relief for RV $\le £12\text{k}$. Linear taper £12,001–£15,000: bill is $(A \times M) / E$ where $E = 3000 / (A - 12000)$ rounded to 3 dec places (+0.001 if remainder > 0.0005) | 12-month grace period on taking additional property (LGFA 1988 s.43(4B); SI 2014/43). SBM threshold: RV $\le £50,999$ | S.I. 2017/411 art 3; S.I. 2017/102 art 3; S.I. 2023/1250 |
| **SSBR** | England | Discretionary cap on bill increases for ratepayers losing/reducing SBRR, Rural Relief, or 2022/23 RHL: £600/yr (2023/24–2025/26), £800/yr (2026/27) | Compounds annually; requires valid prior-year baseline; charities excluded | LGFA 1988 s.47; DLUHC SSBR Guidance |
| **SBRR** | Wales | 100% relief for RV $\le £6\text{k}$. Linear taper £6,001–£12,000: bill is $(A \times M) / E$ where $E = 6000 / (\text{RV} - 6000)$ rounded to 3 dec places. Post offices: 100% $\le £9\text{k}$, 50% £9,001–£12k (Art 4). Childcare: 100% up to £100k RV (Art 5) | Max 2 properties per billing authority (Art 12). Excepted properties (betting shops, payday lenders, part-occupied) excluded (Art 3) | W.S.I. 2017/1229; W.S.I. 2015/976; W.S.I. 2010/248; W.S.I. 2005/309 |
| **SBBS** | Scotland | 100% relief for RV $\le £12\text{k}$; linear taper 100% $\to$ 25% for £12,001–£15,000; linear taper 25% $\to$ 0% for £15,001–£20,000 (Reg 3) | Reg 4 Multiple Properties: cumulative RV $\le £12\text{k}$ grants 100%; cumulative RV £12k–£35k grants 25% for properties $\le £15\text{k}$; cumulative RV > £35k grants 0% across all properties | S.S.I. 2026/39; S.S.I. 2026/68; S.S.I. 2023/34; S.S.I. 2017/36; S.S.I. 2008/106 |
